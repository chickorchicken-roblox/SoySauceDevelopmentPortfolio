Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

The short version: I only collect what I need to talk to you and complete your order. I never sell your information or use it for ads, and this site sets no tracking cookies.

This policy explains how SoySauceDevelopment ("I", "me"), an independent creator based in California, USA, handles personal information when you visit this website or use my services. It's written to meet the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA).

1. Information I collect

Information you give me when you message me, apply for a promotional video or place an order:

  • Your name or username, email address, Discord username and Roblox username
  • Details about your game, links, messages, and any files you send me

Payment information. Card payments are handled by Gumroad. I receive your name, email address and order details from Gumroad, but never your full card number. For Robux payments, Roblox shares your Roblox username and transaction details with me.

Information collected automatically. This website doesn't set cookies or use advertising trackers. The companies that host and deliver it (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and performance. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. If I add privacy-friendly visitor statistics in the future, I'll update this policy first.

2. How I use it

  • To reply to you and discuss your order
  • To make and deliver the work you ordered
  • To process payments and refunds
  • To keep business and tax records, as the law requires
  • To prevent fraud and protect my rights

I do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to send you marketing you didn't ask for.

3. Who I share it with

Only with the services that help me run my work, and only as much as they need:

  • Gumroad: payment processing
  • Roblox: Robux payments
  • Cloudflare and Google: email (routed by Cloudflare, stored in Gmail) and website fonts
  • Discord: if you contact me there
  • GitHub and Cloudflare: website hosting, domain and security

I may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect my rights in a dispute (for example, a chargeback).

4. How long I keep it

I keep your information for as long as I need it to complete your order and support you afterwards. Order and payment records are kept for up to 4 years for tax purposes, then deleted.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, correct it or delete it. Email me and I'll respond within 30 days.
  • I may need to keep some records (such as payment records) where the law requires it.
  • California residents: I don't share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing purposes (California's "Shine the Light" law).

6. Do Not Track

This site doesn't track visitors across other websites, so it works the same way whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

7. Children

My services are not intended for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent me personal information, contact me and I'll delete it. People aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's permission to order.

8. Security

I use reasonable measures to protect your information, such as two-factor authentication on my accounts and trusted payment providers. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, though.

9. Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, such as Gumroad, Roblox and Discord. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

10. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, I'll make that clear on this page.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your information: contact@soysaucedev.com · Discord _juryisout
