02 / the secret sauce

Why it sells

Players don't spend on pretty UI. They spend when the UI gives them a reason. I build in three.

1

Anchor the value

Struck-out prices, multipliers and "best value" ribbons do the math, so players never have to.

2

Show the reward

Big item art, sunbursts and have/need counts make the thing feel real before it's bought.

3

Make it feel good

Buttons that squash, numbers that count up, menus that open like real objects. UI that feels good gets opened again.
