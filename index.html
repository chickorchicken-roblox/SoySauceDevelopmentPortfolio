05 / the secret sauce

Why it works

Detailed UI gives a game its personality. When every screen looks cared for, players feel the game is worth their time.

1

It gives your game a personality

Players spend a lot of time in menus. Custom UI makes your game feel like itself, not a template.

2

It makes playing feel worth it

Polish shows players that someone cared. That trust makes them feel good about playing, and about coming back.

3

It makes every tap feel good

Buttons that squash, numbers that count up, menus that open like real objects. UI that feels good gets opened again.

06 / how a commission brews

Brewed in four steps

01 SteepYour brief, references and what you need: UI, builds, models or SFX. You get a quote and a timeline.
02 FermentA first look (one screen, one area, one model or a few sounds) so we agree on the style before the full build.
03 PressThe full build, made to fit your game, with all the detail and polish.
04 BottlePolish, revisions and a clean handoff your team can extend.
