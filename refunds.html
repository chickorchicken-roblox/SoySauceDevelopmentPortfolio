Refund Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

The short version: you can cancel for a full refund any time before I start. Once I've started, you pay for the work done. If I can't deliver, you get your money back. Refunds are never given just because a review wasn't positive.

Honest Game Review Videos

  • Before I start playing or recording your game: full refund.
  • After I start: no refund, because you're paying for my time and honest opinion.
  • A review being negative or mixed is not a reason for a refund.
  • If I don't deliver the review within 30 days of the estimated date I gave you, you can ask for a full refund.

Promotional Videos

  • Applying is free. If I decline your application, you are never charged.
  • After paying, before I start: full refund.
  • After I start: no refund.
  • If I can't deliver the video, you get a full refund.

Commissions

  • Before work starts: full refund.
  • After work starts: payment for work already completed is non-refundable. Any up-front deposit is non-refundable once work has begun. I may refund the unfinished part of a job at my discretion.
  • If I cancel or can't finish the job, I'll refund the part of your payment that covers unfinished work.
  • Once you've received and accepted the final files, the order is complete and not refundable.

Robux payments

Robux payments are limited by what Roblox's systems allow. Where a refund applies, I'll return it through the same method where possible, and we'll sort out the details together.

How to ask for a refund

Email contact@soysaucedev.com or message me on Discord at _juryisout with your name, what you ordered and the date. I aim to reply within a few days. Approved card refunds are sent through Gumroad to your original payment method. How long it takes to reach you depends on your bank.

Please contact me before filing a chargeback. Most problems can be fixed quickly.
